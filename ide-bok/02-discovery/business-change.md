# Business Change

## Definition

Business Change is the canonical statement of the problem, opportunity, desired outcome, affected stakeholders, value, and success conditions that justify an engineering change.

## Purpose

Preserves the reason for the work before it is translated into software behavior.

## Problem Solved

Requests often arrive as features or tasks without exposing the underlying problem or value.

## Inputs

- Relevant prior concepts, context, evidence, or decisions.

## Outputs

- A governed representation, decision, assessment, or improvement.

## Dependencies

- See related canonical concepts.

## Consumers

- Downstream transformations and governance decisions.

## Quality Criteria

- Clear
- Traceable
- Proportionate to risk
- Technology-independent
- Consistent with the North Star

## Common Mistakes

- Treating the concept as a checklist without understanding its purpose.
- Embedding implementation-specific assumptions in the canonical definition.
- Hiding uncertainty or deviations.
