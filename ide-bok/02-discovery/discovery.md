# Discovery

## Definition

Discovery is the capability that transforms an initial business need into a sufficiently explicit expression of intent, expected behavior, constraints, and success conditions.

## Purpose

Establishes what should change and why before execution decisions begin.

## Problem Solved

Execution often starts from incomplete requests, assumed context, or implementation-first thinking.

## Inputs

- Relevant prior concepts, context, evidence, or decisions.

## Outputs

- A governed representation, decision, assessment, or improvement.

## Consumers
