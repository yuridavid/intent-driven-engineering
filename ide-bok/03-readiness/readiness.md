# Readiness

## Definition

Readiness is the degree to which an intended change has sufficient clarity, context, constraints, dependency understanding, validation strategy, and risk control for a selected execution mode.

## Purpose

Determines whether execution should begin and what conditions must be addressed first.

## Problem Solved

Effort estimation does not determine whether work is understood enough to execute safely.

## Outputs
