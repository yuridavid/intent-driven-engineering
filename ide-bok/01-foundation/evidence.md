# Evidence

## Definition

Evidence is observable information that supports or refutes a claim about correctness, quality, outcome, integrity, or learning.

## Purpose

Replaces confidence, activity, and opinion as the basis for engineering conclusions.

## Problem Solved

Effort, completion, velocity, and implementation volume do not prove that intent was preserved.

## Inputs

- Relevant prior concepts, context, evidence, or decisions.

## Outputs

- A governed representation, decision, assessment, or improvement.
