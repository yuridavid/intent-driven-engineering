# Context

## Definition

Context is the information required to interpret intent correctly and make valid engineering decisions within a specific environment.

## Purpose

Reduces interpretation risk by making rules, constraints, history, assumptions, and dependencies available.

## Problem Solved

The same intent may produce different and incorrect decisions when relevant conditions remain hidden.

## Inputs

- Relevant prior concepts, context, evidence, or decisions.

## Outputs

- A governed representation, decision, assessment, or improvement.
