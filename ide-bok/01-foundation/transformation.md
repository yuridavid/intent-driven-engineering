# Transformation

## Definition

A transformation is the conversion of one representation of intent into another representation closer to validated operational behavior.

## Purpose

Makes the engineering chain visible and governable.

## Problem Solved

Meaning loss between business understanding, specification, execution, validation, and operation is otherwise difficult to detect.
