# Intent

## Definition

Intent is the meaning, outcome, behavior, constraint, or value that an engineering change is expected to preserve or produce.

## Purpose

Provides the reason and direction for engineering work.

## Problem Solved

Implementation may be technically correct while solving the wrong problem or violating important constraints.

## Inputs

- Relevant prior concepts, context, evidence, or decisions.

## Outputs

- A governed representation, decision, assessment, or improvement.

- Traceable
- Proportionate to risk
- Technology-independent
