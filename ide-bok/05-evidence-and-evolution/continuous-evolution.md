# Continuous Evolution

## Definition

Continuous Evolution is the capability that converts engineering and operational evidence into improvements to discovery, readiness, execution boundaries, standards, templates, policies, product decisions, and the Body of Knowledge.

## Purpose

Closes the loop so each completed transformation improves future transformations.

## Problem Solved

Organizations collect data but often fail to convert it into explicit changes to the engineering system.

## Inputs

- Relevant prior concepts, context, evidence, or decisions.

## Outputs

- A governed representation, decision, assessment, or improvement.
