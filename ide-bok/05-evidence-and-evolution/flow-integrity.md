# Flow Integrity

## Definition

Flow Integrity is the degree to which business intent remains preserved across the complete chain of engineering transformations.

## Purpose

Verifies that operational behavior still represents the original Business Change and approved Source Specification.

## Problem Solved

Individual stages may appear successful while the end-to-end result silently diverges from intent.
