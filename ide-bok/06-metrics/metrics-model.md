# Metrics Model

## Principle

Metrics exist to improve decisions, not to create the appearance of control. No single metric represents engineering performance.

## Families

### Outcome Metrics
Show whether the intended business or user outcome occurred.

### Quality Metrics
Show whether expected behavior and constraints were preserved.

### Flow Metrics
Show how effectively intent moves through the engineering system.

### Execution Health Metrics
Show the reliability and efficiency of execution capabilities.

### Context Health Metrics
Show whether required knowledge is available, current, and consistent.

## Mandatory metric metadata

Every metric must include:

- purpose;
- calculation;
- source;
- interpretation;
- limitations;
- gaming risks;
- unsafe conclusions;
- related decisions.

Activity volume must not automatically be treated as value, quality, or productivity.
