# Knowledge Taxonomy

## Foundational Knowledge
Enduring identity, principles, and invariants. Rarely changes.

## Core Knowledge
Principal capabilities and relationships, such as Discovery, Readiness, Execution Boundary, Flow Integrity, and Continuous Evolution. Changes deliberately.

## Operational Knowledge
Templates, examples, checklists, playbooks, reference cards, and operational metrics. Changes frequently.

## Experimental Knowledge
Observations, hypotheses, comparisons, prototypes, and unresolved ideas. Changes freely.

## Promotion path

```text
Observation
    ↓
Hypothesis
    ↓
Evaluation
    ↓
Evidence Review
    ↓
Concept Proposal
    ↓
Governance Review
    ↓
Canonical Knowledge
```

Rejected hypotheses remain recorded with reason, evidence, date, affected concepts, and reconsideration conditions.
