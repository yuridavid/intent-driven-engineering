# IDE-BoK Glossary

Canonical concept files remain authoritative.

- **Business Change:** Statement of the problem, opportunity, desired outcome, stakeholders, value, and success conditions that justify an engineering change.
- **Context:** Information required to interpret intent correctly in a specific environment.
- **Controlled Transformation:** Change between engineering representations in which inputs, outputs, decisions, validation, and evidence are explicit.
- **Discovery:** Capability that transforms an initial need into explicit intent and expected behavior.
- **Evidence:** Observable information supporting or refuting a claim about correctness, quality, outcome, or integrity.
- **Execution Boundary:** Interface between preparation and execution defining scope, permissions, constraints, validation, evidence, and escalation.
- **Flow Integrity:** Degree to which business intent remains preserved across the engineering chain.
- **Intent:** Meaning, outcome, behavior, constraint, or value that a change is expected to preserve or produce.
- **Readiness:** Degree to which a change has sufficient clarity, context, constraints, dependency understanding, validation strategy, and risk control for a selected execution mode.
- **Source Specification:** Canonical expression of expected behavior, rules, constraints, assumptions, edge cases, errors, and acceptance conditions.
- **Transformation:** Conversion of one representation of intent into another closer to operational behavior.
- **Uncertainty:** Degree to which interpretation, execution, outcome, impact, or validation remains unknown.
- **Validation:** Process of obtaining evidence that a representation, transformation, or behavior satisfies intended conditions.
