# Research Promotion Process

## Stages

1. Observation
2. Hypothesis
3. Evaluation
4. Evidence Review
5. Concept Proposal
6. Impact Analysis
7. Governance Decision
8. Promotion

## Promotion criteria

A proposal must:

- solve a distinct problem;
- have sufficient evidence;
- strengthen coherence;
- preserve simplicity;
- support the North Star;
- have understandable boundaries;
- avoid technology dependence unless operational.

## Decisions

- Accepted
- Accepted with revision
- Retained as experimental
- Rejected
- Deferred

Rejected and superseded proposals remain available for historical learning.
