# Ownership Model

- **Repository Owner:** access, publication, licensing, and final governance authority.
- **IDE-BoK Steward:** protects Constitution, taxonomy, terminology, and coherence; owns cross-cutting architecture coherence, periodic review, impact assessment, and coordination of breaking architectural changes with the Repository Owner.
- **Domain Steward:** maintains a specific domain.
- **Research Contributor:** creates hypotheses, comparisons, experiments, and evidence.
- **Reviewer:** evaluates clarity, evidence, duplication, impact, and constitutional alignment.

During the initial stage, one person may hold multiple roles, but decisions should still be documented.
